Answers to your questions

Is it painful?
Laser epilation is practically painless.
How many sessions?
Usually 6–10 laser sessions every 4–8 weeks.
Can I epilate with a tan?
Avoid sun for 4 weeks before. Use SPF after.
How long is one session?
Underarms 15–20 min, legs 40–60 min, full body 90–120 min.
When will I see results?
20–30% less hair after the first session.
How to prepare?
Shave 2–3 days before. No waxing.

Laser epilation

Laser epilation is the most effective method for permanent removal of dark hair. The diode laser targets melanin, destroys the follicle with thermal energy without damaging surrounding skin.

Book now from 20 € First consultation free

How does it work?

The laser beam is absorbed by melanin in the hair. Thermal energy destroys the follicle, preventing further growth.

Who is it for?

Ideal for dark hair on light skin. For light, red or grey hair we recommend electro-epilation.

Session overview

15–60 minutes depending on the zone. Feels like a rubber band snap. We recommend 6–10 sessions with 4–8 week intervals.

Preparation & aftercare

Shave the zone the day before. Avoid sun 2 weeks before and after. Apply soothing cream after each session.

FAQ

Does laser epilation hurt?
The sensation is like a rubber band snap. Modern devices have cooling that significantly reduces discomfort.
How many sessions do I need?
On average 6–10 sessions depending on the zone and hair type.
How long do results last?
After completing the course results are permanent. Hormonal changes may require one correction session.
When will I see first results?
After 2–3 sessions significant reduction. After the full course — up to 90% permanent removal.

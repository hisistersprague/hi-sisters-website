Laser epilation

Do you guarantee the result?

Yes — if laser hair removal is right for you. At the consultation we look at the type and colour of your hair, the condition of your skin, and whether hormones are driving the growth. If the method suits you, we see the course through to the result. If it does not, we say so immediately and offer electrolysis, which works on hair of any colour.

Does laser hair removal hurt?

It feels like a short hot flick. The cooled tip softens it noticeably. The most sensitive areas are those with thin skin — bikini and underarms; the first sessions are sharper, then it gets easier as there is less hair.

How many sessions will I need?

Usually 6–10, sometimes more: the laser only works on hair in the active growth phase and that share differs from person to person, so we do not quote a fixed number in advance. Thinning is usually visible after 2–3 sessions, and we guarantee the result if the method suits you.

Why do the intervals between sessions get longer?

Because each time there is less hair left, and the remaining hairs need more time to enter the active phase. Coming in early means the session is largely wasted.

Is the result permanent?

A treated follicle does not recover. But the skin holds follicles that were dormant during the course, and hormones can switch them on — pregnancy, thyroid, medication. Then single maintenance sessions are enough, not a new course.

Do you treat a woman’s face with laser?

No. Facial hair in women is more often hormone-driven, and the laser can stimulate growth in that area. For the face we offer electrolysis, which treats each hair individually.

Can it be done in summer?

Yes, if the area is covered by clothing and you are not tanning. For exposed areas — legs, arms, shoulders — summer means SPF and no tanning for two weeks before and after a session.

What about ingrown hairs?

They usually go along with the course: the hair stops growing, so there is nothing to grow in. Early in the course a gentle scrub helps, as does swapping the razor for a trimmer between sessions.

Electrolysis

Does it hurt?

It feels like a short pinpoint sting with warmth. Tolerance varies: the body is easier, the face and bikini more sensitive. We use topical numbing and set the parameters to your threshold. It is not a painless method, and we say so upfront.

How many sessions will I need?

The face usually takes 10–20 sessions and the body 15–30 or more, spaced 1–4 weeks apart. The exact number depends on the area, hair density, hormones and whether the area was plucked in the past. It is a guide, not a fixed promise.

Can blond and grey hair be removed?

Yes. Electrolysis does not depend on pigment: the needle works on the follicle itself, so hair colour is irrelevant. That is the key difference from laser.

Will there be crusts?

There can be — a normal part of healing, particularly on the face and with coarse hair. They clear in 5–10 days and must not be picked. Promising no crusts at all would not be honest: skin reacts differently in everyone.

Will it leave marks or scars?

Not with correct parameters and aftercare. The risk rises if crusts are picked, if fresh skin is tanned, or if the work is done on an inflamed area — which is why we do not treat an irritated area.

Do I pay per area or per time?

Per time — by the minute, on the device timer, for the time actually worked. It is fairer: the same area holds a different amount of hair on different people.

Can it be combined with laser?

Yes, and it often is: the laser clears the bulk of the dark hair, electrolysis handles the blond, grey and remaining single hairs. We set the order at the consultation.

Trichology

Why does hair fall out?

There are many causes and usually several at once: iron and vitamin D deficiency, thyroid problems, hormonal change including after childbirth, stress and poor sleep, strict diets, recent illness, certain medications, and the condition of the scalp itself. That is why we start with an examination rather than by booking treatments.

When should I see a trichologist?

If heavier shedding has lasted more than a month, if volume has visibly dropped, if there is itching, flaking or crusting, or if shedding started after childbirth, illness or rapid weight loss. The sooner the better: holding on to density is easier than bringing back follicles that are already gone.

Do I need blood tests?

Not always, but often. If trichoscopy shows a picture typical of deficiencies or hormonal causes, going further without tests is pointless. We will say which ones at the appointment; usually ferritin, vitamin D, thyroid markers and a full blood count.

When can I expect results?

The first changes show after 2–3 months, a clear result after 4–6. That is not the clinic being slow — it is the hair cycle: new hair grows about a centimetre a month. Anyone promising results in two weeks is talking about how the lengths look, not about the roots.

Will scalp mesotherapy help?

It works well as part of a plan once the cause is clear. As the only measure against iron deficiency or a thyroid problem it gives a temporary effect and the shedding returns. That is why we prescribe it after an examination rather than on request.

Should I wash my hair before the appointment?

Come with your hair in its usual state — better not to wash it on the day. That way the real condition of the scalp, the oiliness at the roots and any flaking are visible.

Consultations

Cosmetologist consultation with skin analysis

60 minutes · 1000 Kč

Every plan starts here: first we look at the actual state of your skin, and only then decide which treatments you need and in what order.

The cosmetologist examines your skin, assesses its type and its condition on the day, and notes the areas with breakouts, enlarged pores, pigmentation or dryness. We also go through what you use at home — home care is behind about half of the problems clients come in with.

You leave with a clear plan: which treatments you need, how far apart to space them, what to drop from your home routine and what to add. If your skin is not in a state for a treatment right now, we will say so plainly and suggest waiting.

Who it suits

  • you are seeing a cosmetologist for the first time and do not know where to start
  • your skin has changed: breakouts, dryness or pigmentation have appeared
  • your home routine has stopped working, or you are not sure it suits you
  • you want to know whether you need a course of treatments or a single one is enough

How it goes

  1. a conversation: what bothers you, what you have tried, your conditions and medications
  2. examining the skin under a lamp: skin type, pores, breakouts, pigmentation
  3. going through your home routine — bring the products or photos of them
  4. the plan: treatments, intervals, home care

After the treatment

  • your skin is unchanged — this is an assessment, not a treatment
  • if you want to start right away, some treatments can be done the same day
  • the consultation fee counts towards a treatment done on the same day

How often. A follow-up makes sense every 3–6 months, or whenever your skin changes noticeably — after a change of climate, a pregnancy or a new course of medication.

Dermatologist consultation

45 minutes · 1500 Kč

This is for questions that go beyond cosmetology: a skin condition, prescriptions, tests.

A cosmetologist works with the condition of your skin; a doctor works with disease. If we see moderate or severe acne, rosacea, dermatitis, suspicious lesions or a reaction to medication, it is more honest to send you to a dermatologist than to paper over it with skincare treatments.

The doctor can prescribe treatment and order tests, after which we calmly return to skincare. This split saves money: some problems are solved by medicine, not by a course of treatments.

Who it suits

  • acne that skincare treatments are not resolving
  • rosacea, dermatitis, flare-ups and itching
  • new or changed moles and other lesions
  • heavy hair loss together with scalp problems

How it goes

  1. history: when it started, how it was treated, what you take
  2. examination of the skin, with dermatoscopy if needed
  3. a plan: treatment, tests or referral to another specialist
  4. agreeing which cosmetology treatments can run alongside

After the treatment

  • nothing changes on the skin — this is a medical appointment
  • if treatment is prescribed, we choose skincare treatments around it
  • some treatments will have to wait while you are being treated — we say so upfront

How often. As needed: during active treatment, as the doctor schedules it; moles are worth checking once a year.

Facial cleansing

Device-assisted facial cleansing

60 minutes · 1000 Kč

The gentlest of the cleansings: ultrasound only, no extractions. It suits sensitive skin with a moderate number of clogged pores.

An ultrasonic spatula lifts off dead cells and draws the contents of the pores out through vibration and water — the skin is not traumatised or stretched. Afterwards the face barely reddens, so this one fits before an event or over a lunch break.

The honest limitation: ultrasound does not reach deep closed comedones or firm plugs. If the cosmetologist sees a lot of them, she will suggest mechanical or combined cleansing instead — otherwise you pay for a treatment that only half-solves the problem.

Who it suits

  • sensitive skin that reddens easily
  • a modest number of open comedones, enlarged pores
  • you need a refresh with no visible traces
  • a first cleansing if you are wary of painful treatments

How it goes

  1. make-up removal and cleansing
  2. a softening solution so the pores open
  3. ultrasonic cleansing area by area
  4. a soothing mask and a cream matched to your skin

After the treatment

  • slight redness settles within half an hour to an hour
  • make-up is fine the same day if the skin is calm
  • no sauna, pool or hard training for a day
  • an SPF cream is a must, especially in spring and summer

How often. Every 3–4 weeks for oily skin, every 6–8 weeks for normal skin. More often is pointless: the pores have not refilled and the skin barrier suffers.

Mechanical facial cleansing

90 minutes · 1500 Kč

The classic deep cleanse: the cosmetologist clears comedones and plugs by hand. It does what a device cannot reach, but it needs recovery time.

The skin is steamed or softened with a dedicated solution, then the cosmetologist clears closed and open comedones, milia and firm plugs with a sterile tool and gloved fingers. It is meticulous, area-by-area work — which is why the appointment runs about an hour and a half.

Mechanical cleansing removes the most, but it is also the most visible on the skin: redness lasts from a few hours up to a day and small pinpoint crusts can appear. We do not schedule it right before an event, and we say plainly that badly congested skin is not brought to perfection in one go: it usually takes 2–3 sessions a month apart plus a home routine that actually works.

Who it suits

  • plenty of closed comedones and blackheads
  • thick, oily skin with enlarged pores
  • milia that nothing else shifts
  • skin after a long gap without care

How it goes

  1. cleansing and degreasing the skin
  2. softening: steam or cold hydration with a solution
  3. manual clearing of comedones area by area, with a tool and gloved hands
  4. disinfection, a pore-tightening mask, a soothing cream

After the treatment

  • redness and slight swelling — from a few hours up to a day
  • pinpoint crusts can form where deep comedones were: do not pick them
  • two or three days without make-up on the treated spots, and no sauna, pool or sport
  • SPF every day: freshly cleared skin pigments easily
  • if you use acids or retinol at home, pause them for 3–5 days

How often. For actively congested skin: 2–3 sessions 3–4 weeks apart, then maintenance every 1.5–2 months. On calm skin, more often than once a month is not worth it.

Combined facial cleansing

120 minutes · 1700 Kč

Ultrasound plus hands: the device clears the surface, the hands reach what sits deep. This is the one we recommend most often.

First the ultrasound lifts the keratinised layer and everything sitting shallow in the pores, which cuts down the amount of manual work. Then the cosmetologist clears by hand what ultrasound cannot take: closed comedones, milia, firm plugs in the T-zone.

The difference from mechanical cleansing is the load on the skin. Because the device does part of the job, the hands work less and more selectively, so redness usually settles faster while pore clarity is comparable. The difference from device-only cleansing is depth: comedones out of ultrasound's reach do get removed here.

Who it suits

  • combination skin: oily T-zone, dry cheeks
  • comedones both on the surface and deeper down
  • you want the result of a mechanical cleanse with less redness
  • regular care for skin prone to breakouts

How it goes

  1. make-up removal, cleansing, degreasing
  2. softening the skin
  3. the ultrasound stage across the whole face
  4. manual work on the problem areas
  5. disinfection, a mask for your skin type, a protective cream

After the treatment

  • redness usually clears within a few hours, occasionally lasting until morning
  • pinpoint crusts are possible where deep comedones were removed
  • a day or two without sauna, pool or intense training
  • better to return to make-up the next day
  • SPF daily; acids and retinol after 3–5 days

How often. Every 4–6 weeks if you are prone to breakouts, every 2–3 months for maintenance. We set the interval by how fast your pores refill, not by the calendar.

DMK protocol facial cleansing

120 minutes · 2500 Kč

Cleansing the DMK way: enzymes first soften what sits in the pores and get the circulation going, and only then does the cosmetologist clear the comedones.

The protocol is built on enzyme preparation. An enzyme-based formula works on the surface keratinised cells and increases blood flow, so plugs come out more easily and less mechanical pressure is needed. After that comes the usual area-by-area manual work and a finishing mask.

We do not lump all DMK treatments together: the brand has its own logic, and cleansing is a separate step within it — not the same thing as Fire & Ice or an enzyme mask. Which protocol you need is decided at the consultation, based on the state of your skin on the day rather than on what is trending.

Who it suits

  • congested skin that tolerates a plain mechanical cleanse poorly
  • a dull complexion, slowed skin renewal
  • you are already on DMK care and want to stay within one system
  • oily skin with stagnant breakouts

How it goes

  1. cleansing and preparing the skin with the line's products
  2. the enzyme stage: the formula stays on the skin for a set time
  3. manual clearing of comedones and plugs area by area
  4. a finishing mask and home-care guidance within the DMK system

After the treatment

  • during the enzyme stage the skin can feel noticeably tight and tingly — that is expected
  • redness lasts a few hours, sometimes until the next morning
  • no sauna, pool or hard training for a day
  • SPF is a must, particularly if peels are planned
  • it is worth aligning your home routine: the system is built on consistency

How often. Usually as a course: 3–4 sessions 2–4 weeks apart, then maintenance. The exact interval depends on how your skin responds to the enzyme stage.

Peels

Quick Peel

45 minutes · 1500 Kč

A short superficial peel: it refreshes the complexion and evens out texture without disrupting your week.

The formula works within the upper layers of the skin: it speeds up exfoliation, so the face looks fresher, pores read cleaner and fine unevenness smooths out. This is genuinely the quick option — often chosen as a first peel or as a top-up between heavier treatments.

A superficial peel will not remove strong pigmentation, scars or deep wrinkles. If that is the goal, it is more honest to discuss a course or other treatments right away — Quick Peel alone will not get you there.

Who it suits

  • a dull complexion, skin that looks tired
  • uneven texture, enlarged pores
  • a first experience of peeling
  • you want a quick treatment with no downtime

How it goes

  1. cleansing and preparing the skin
  2. applying the formula for a set time, area by area
  3. neutralising
  4. soothing care and SPF

After the treatment

  • mild stinging and tingling during application is normal
  • redness usually passes within 1–2 hours
  • light flaking, most often on days 2–3
  • SPF is a must every day afterwards
  • acids, scrubs and retinol at home — pause for 5–7 days

How often. As a course of 4–6 sessions 7–14 days apart, then according to your skin. We leave a gap between courses: constant exfoliation thins the protective barrier.

PMP peel

60 minutes · 2500 Kč

A peel aimed at tone and skin density: for when a single superficial treatment is no longer enough.

We tailor the session to the goal: uneven tone, post-acne marks, dullness, loss of skin density. The cosmetologist controls the contact time and the number of layers — both the result and the skin's reaction depend on this, so the parameters are always individual.

This peel works cumulatively: a visible difference usually comes after 3–4 sessions, not after the first. We say so at the consultation so expectations match reality.

Who it suits

  • uneven tone and marks left by breakouts
  • skin that has lost density and freshness
  • enlarged pores and uneven texture
  • you need a course rather than a one-off

How it goes

  1. assessment and choice of parameters for your skin
  2. cleansing and degreasing
  3. applying the formula in layers while watching the reaction
  4. neutralising and restorative care
  5. SPF and home-care guidance

After the treatment

  • redness and a feeling of tightness on day one
  • flaking from day 2–4, sometimes noticeable: that is an expected part of it
  • do not pick the flaking skin and do not use scrubs
  • SPF every day, or pigmentation can follow
  • sport, sauna and pool after 3–4 days

How often. A course of 3–6 sessions 2–4 weeks apart, mostly in autumn and winter. In summer or strong sun we usually postpone the course.

JALUPRO GLOW PEEL

60 minutes · 2500 Kč

A peel focused on glow: chosen when you need the face refreshed before an event without heavy flaking.

The treatment is gentle: exfoliation is paired with a hydrating, restorative stage, so the skin looks nourished rather than stripped. This is the peel most often chosen before a shoot, a wedding or a trip.

Glow is not a substitute for a treatment course. With pronounced pigmentation or post-acne marks, the cosmetologist will suggest combining this peel with other treatments rather than repeating it indefinitely.

Who it suits

  • you need the face refreshed a few days before an event
  • dry, dehydrated skin
  • a dull tone without pronounced problems
  • a first peel for sensitive skin

How it goes

  1. cleansing and preparation
  2. the peeling stage with timed control
  3. neutralising
  4. hydrating and restorative care
  5. SPF

After the treatment

  • redness is mild and usually gone the same day
  • flaking is fine or absent altogether
  • make-up is fine the next day
  • SPF daily
  • before an important event, book it 3–5 days ahead, not the night before

How often. As a one-off before an event, or as a course of 3–4 sessions 10–14 days apart.

Cryo Pro X peel

60 minutes · 1700 Kč

A peel with a cooling action: exfoliation happens cold, so it is easier to tolerate than the description suggests.

The formula combines acid exfoliation with cooling: the skin renews while taking less irritation than with a standard acid peel. That makes it an option for reactive skin that flushes quickly.

Like any peel, it needs sun protection and a pause in aggressive home care. If the skin is inflamed or damaged on the day, we reschedule — that is not politeness, it is a condition for the result.

Who it suits

  • reactive skin that tolerates ordinary acids poorly
  • uneven tone and dullness
  • enlarged pores, oily shine
  • you want renewal without obvious flaking

How it goes

  1. cleansing and degreasing
  2. applying the cooling peel formula
  3. contact time under the cosmetologist's control
  4. removing the formula, soothing care
  5. SPF and guidance

After the treatment

  • a sensation of cold and tingling during the session
  • redness usually settles within a few hours
  • light flaking is possible on days 2–3
  • SPF daily
  • acids and retinol at home after 5–7 days

How often. A course of 4–6 sessions 10–14 days apart, with a 2–3 month gap between courses.

Fire & Ice by DMK

90 minutes · 3000 Kč

A two-phase treatment: an active phase with noticeable heat, then a cooling, restorative one. Hence the name.

In the first phase the formula heats the skin markedly and boosts circulation — that is what drives the renewal. In the second phase the skin is cooled and calmed, and the reaction subsides. The sensations in phase one are strong: heat, prickling, sometimes the urge to stop. The cosmetologist works to the clock and can always slow things down.

This is a distinct DMK protocol, not the DMK cleansing and not a mask: they serve different purposes. Fire & Ice is chosen to improve the look of the skin quickly and tighten texture, including before an event. We do not promise a laser-like effect — that is a different level of intervention.

Who it suits

  • you want a fast visible result in texture and tone
  • uneven skin, enlarged pores
  • post-acne marks without active inflammation
  • you already know DMK treatments

How it goes

  1. cleansing and preparing the skin
  2. phase one: the active formula, noticeable heat
  3. phase two: cooling and recovery
  4. finishing care and SPF

After the treatment

  • phase one brings pronounced heat and prickling — that is part of it
  • redness lasts from a few hours up to a day
  • light flaking is possible on days 2–4
  • no sauna, pool or sport for a day
  • SPF is a must, especially in the first days
  • do not book it the day before something important

How often. As a one-off or a course of 3–4 sessions 2–4 weeks apart. Not more often: the skin needs time between active protocols.

Skincare treatments

Carboxytherapy

60 minutes · 1500 Kč

A non-injection treatment: carbon dioxide wakes the skin up by increasing the flow of blood and oxygen.

A mask and an activating formula are applied; together they release carbon dioxide. The skin responds by widening its capillaries — blood and oxygen flow increase, the face flushes pink and you feel warmth and a light tingle. The complexion improves and the skin looks denser and better nourished.

Carboxytherapy works well as preparation for other treatments and as care for tired, dehydrated skin. It is not a replacement for injectables: it does not fill wrinkles or change the contour, but it noticeably refreshes the skin and makes it look alive.

Who it suits

  • dull, tired skin with a greyish tone
  • dehydration and a feeling of tightness
  • preparing the skin before an event
  • skin after flights, poor sleep, stress

How it goes

  1. cleansing the skin
  2. applying the active formula and the mask
  3. contact time: the skin turns pink, you feel warmth and tingling
  4. removing the mask, hydrating care and SPF

After the treatment

  • the pink flush fades in 20–40 minutes
  • make-up is fine the same day
  • there are practically no sauna or sport restrictions
  • SPF as usual for the season

How often. As a course of 5–10 sessions 3–7 days apart, then maintenance once a month. As a one-off, a refresher before an event.

Enzyme therapy

90 minutes · 2200 Kč

A treatment with an enzyme mask that tightens the skin noticeably and gets the blood moving. One of the most sensation-heavy — and most visible — treatments we do.

The enzyme mask sets on the face and works for about 40–45 minutes. During that time you feel strong tightening, pulsing and warmth — the skin is being pushed by the increased blood flow. That is a normal reaction and we warn about it in advance: this is not a relaxing treatment, and lying still under that mask does not suit everyone.

Once the mask is off, the skin looks denser, smoother and fresher, and puffiness goes down. It is often taken as a course for dullness, loss of tone and post-acne marks, and as an alternative to injectables for those not ready for them.

Who it suits

  • loss of tone and skin density
  • a dull complexion, puffiness
  • post-acne marks and uneven tone
  • you are not ready for injectables but want a visible result

How it goes

  1. cleansing and preparing the skin
  2. applying the enzyme mask
  3. 40–45 minutes of contact: tightening, pulsing, warmth
  4. removing the mask, restorative care
  5. SPF and home-care guidance

After the treatment

  • marked redness for the first 1–3 hours — that is expected
  • the skin sometimes flakes slightly a day or two later
  • no sauna, pool or hard training for a day
  • SPF is a must
  • better not to book it the day before something important

How often. A course of 4–6 sessions 1–2 weeks apart, then maintenance every 4–6 weeks.

Enzyme therapy with Quick Peel

90 minutes · 2500 Kč

The same enzyme therapy, but with a peel first: we lift the keratinised layer, then work with the mask.

A peel before the enzyme mask removes dead cells and makes the skin more receptive — the mask works deeper and the gain in tone and density is more visible. The logic is simple: the better the skin is prepared, the more the active stage gives back.

The combination is more active than either treatment alone, so the skin's reaction is stronger too: redness lasts longer and flaking is possible. The cosmetologist decides at the appointment whether your skin can take that load today.

Who it suits

  • thicker skin with uneven tone
  • post-acne marks
  • you want a stronger result than the mask alone gives
  • you have had enzyme therapy before and tolerated it well

How it goes

  1. cleansing and degreasing
  2. the peel stage and neutralising
  3. applying the enzyme mask and letting it work
  4. removing the mask, restorative care
  5. SPF and guidance for the next few days

After the treatment

  • pronounced redness for several hours, sometimes until the next morning
  • flaking is possible on days 2–4
  • 2–3 days without sauna, pool or sport
  • SPF daily, acids at home after 5–7 days
  • better to wait a day before make-up

How often. A course of 4–6 sessions two weeks apart, alternated with calmer treatments so the skin is not overloaded.

Device-based treatments

Microcurrent

60 minutes · from 1800 Kč

A low-current treatment: it works on the muscles and lymph flow, reduces puffiness and brings tone back to the face. It feels like a light tingle.

Electrodes pass a weak pulsed current over the face that the skin barely registers. The current works on the mimic muscles and improves lymphatic drainage: puffiness goes, the face looks more defined, the contour sharper. The session is comfortable, with no redness and no downtime, so it fits any day.

The honest part about microcurrent: the effect is cumulative and needs a course. A single session gives a visible but short-lived result — enough before a shoot, for instance. Holding the tone takes a course and then maintenance; we do not promise a lift comparable to heat-based devices, because the mechanism is different.

Who it suits

  • puffiness, especially in the mornings
  • loss of tone, a tired-looking face
  • you need a fast result before an event
  • recovery after a long stretch of stress or poor sleep
  • preparing the skin for other treatments

How it goes

  1. cleansing the skin
  2. applying conductive gel
  3. working with the electrodes across the face and neck
  4. finishing care matched to your skin

After the treatment

  • no traces: you can put make-up on and carry on with your day
  • no restrictions on sport, sauna or pool
  • the effect of a single session lasts a few days
  • not performed with a pacemaker, epilepsy, pregnancy or metal implants in the treated area — we ask about this beforehand

How often. A course of 5–15 sessions, 2–3 times a week, then maintenance every 2–4 weeks. Packages of 5, 10 and 15 work out cheaper than single sessions — see the price list above.

Facial massage

Facial massage

90 minutes · from 1500 Kč

An hour and a half of hands-on work: face, neck, décolleté. It releases tension, reduces puffiness and visibly changes the complexion.

Massage works on what rarely gets attention: tension in the mimic and chewing muscles, stagnant lymph, tightness in the neck. Afterwards the morning puffiness usually goes, the forehead and the area between the brows relax, and the face looks rested.

It works best as a course and alongside other treatments: massage amplifies skincare results because it improves blood supply to the skin. The effect is clear but not permanent — muscles return to their habitual tone, so a maintenance rhythm makes more sense than a single session.

Who it suits

  • puffiness and a heavy-looking face in the mornings
  • neck tension, a clenched jaw, a habit of frowning
  • loss of skin tone
  • you want results without devices or injections
  • course-based care alongside other treatments

How it goes

  1. make-up removal and cleansing
  2. massage of the face, neck and décolleté — about 90 minutes
  3. muscle work and lymphatic drainage
  4. finishing care matched to your skin

After the treatment

  • light redness from the warming fades within half an hour
  • a little sleepiness afterwards is normal
  • make-up is fine the same day
  • no sport or sauna restrictions
  • we do not massage over active breakouts or inflammation

How often. A course of 6–15 sessions, once or twice a week, then maintenance every 2–4 weeks. Packages of 6, 8, 10 and 15 are better value than single sessions — see the price list above.

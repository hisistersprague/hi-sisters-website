DMK mask

A short treatment within the DMK system: a maintenance step between the main protocols.

Book now 45 minutes · 1000 Kč First consultation free

The mask is chosen for the day's goal: to calm the skin, hydrate it, or hold the result after a cleanse or an active protocol. It is not a standalone cure but part of a system — so it is usually done alongside other DMK treatments or between them.

The session is calm, with no downtime: afterwards the skin looks more even and better hydrated, with no redness or flaking.

Who it suits

  • maintenance between the main treatments
  • skin after a cleanse or an active protocol
  • dehydration and a feeling of tightness
  • you want a calm treatment with no downtime

How it goes

  1. cleansing the skin
  2. choosing the mask for the skin's condition
  3. application and contact time
  4. finishing care and SPF

After the treatment

  • practically no restrictions
  • make-up is fine straight away
  • SPF as usual for the season

How often. As your care plan dictates: usually between the main treatments, every 1–2 weeks.

Each service in detail

Laser hair removal

Removes dark, coarse hair. Power and pulse length are set to your skin phototype and hair thickness, which is why we never promise “five sessions for everyone”.

  • The first gap is about a month, then intervals grow to two or three
  • A course is usually 4–8 sessions; we name the number after examining you
  • Laser does not work on light, red or grey hair — electrolysis does

Electrolysis

The only method that removes hair of any colour — blond, red or grey. You pay by the minute, on the machine’s own timer, for the time actually worked.

  • Can be done with 4, 6 or 8 hands — an area is finished several times faster
  • Topical anaesthetic on request; you can apply it before you arrive
  • For areas where laser cannot help, and for single hairs after a laser course

Answers to your questions

Is it painful?
Laser epilation is practically painless.
How many sessions?
Usually 6–10 laser sessions every 4–8 weeks.
Can I epilate with a tan?
Avoid sun for 4 weeks before. Use SPF after.
How long is one session?
Underarms 15–20 min, legs 40–60 min, full body 90–120 min.
When will I see results?
20–30% less hair after the first session.
How to prepare?
Shave 2–3 days before. No waxing.

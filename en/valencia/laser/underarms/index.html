How many sessions it takes

Underarms usually clear in 6–8 treatments. The hair here is dark and dense, so the laser works on it particularly well.

Why the intervals are long

The second session comes two months after the first, or later. The first year of a course means 4–5 visits, not a trip every month.

What it feels like

Short, noticeable tingling. The area is small, so it is over quickly. Burning should not happen — it is a sign the settings are wrong.

What to avoid afterwards

No deodorant for a day, no sauna or pool for two days, no sunbathing for two weeks. Short rules, but they decide whether you get irritation.

Answers to your questions

Can it be done in summer?
Yes, as long as you stay out of the sun for two weeks before and after and use sun protection.
Why does the hair not fall out straight away?
A destroyed hair leaves the skin on day 10–14 after the session. That is normal and expected.

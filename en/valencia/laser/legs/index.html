Laser hair removal, legs in Valencia

Shins, thighs or full legs. A large area — which is exactly where the right interval saves you the most visits.

How many sessions it takes

Legs usually take 6–10 treatments. Shins respond faster than thighs: the hair there is denser and darker.

A package costs less

Legs are often taken together with underarms and bikini — in a package the area comes out cheaper than separately. Current packages are in the price list.

What it feels like

A noticeable tingling, slightly stronger on the inner thighs. Burning should not happen: the settings are chosen for your skin and change from session to session.

How to prepare

Shave your legs a day before. No sunbathing for two weeks. No waxing or plucking for a month — the laser only works on hair still in the follicle.

Laser hair removal for men in Valencia

Back, chest, shoulders, neck, arms. Male hair is thicker and denser than female hair — the laser works particularly well on it, but the settings have to be different.

Book now from 35 € First consultation free

In short

  • Works on dark, dense hair: the laser acts through melanin.
  • A course is usually 6–10 sessions, with intervals growing from 4 to 8 weeks.
  • A woman’s face we treat with electrolysis, not laser.
  • From 35 €. First consultation free.

The areas men ask for most

Back and shoulders come first: you cannot shave them yourself and the hair grows back fast. Then chest and abdomen, the neck and the beard line, and arms. We treat all of these, including a full torso or a full back in one session.

How many sessions

Usually 8–12 — more than for women: male hair is denser, and on the back and chest it responds more to hormones. Visible thinning shows after 3–4 sessions.

What it feels like

Male skin is thicker, and the power is set accordingly. It feels like a short hot flick; the back takes it more easily than the chest and abdomen.

Preparation and aftercare

Shave the area a day before, no tanning for two weeks, no creams or deodorant on the day. Afterwards: no sauna, pool or gym for a day, SPF on exposed skin.

Answers to your questions

Can I keep the beard and clear only the neck?
Yes, that is a common request. We agree the beard line beforehand and treat only below it, so the edge stays clean.
Will back hair come back?
A treated follicle does not recover. Growth on the back and chest depends on hormones, though, so single hairs can appear over time — one maintenance session handles them.
How long does a back session take?
A full back is about 30–40 minutes. A full torso takes longer; we will give you the exact time at the consultation.

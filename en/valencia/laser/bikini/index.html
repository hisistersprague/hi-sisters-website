How many sessions it takes

A course usually runs to 6–10 treatments. The exact number depends on your hormones and on the type and density of the hair — we tell you at the free consultation, before you pay for the first session.

Why there are two months in between

The laser only works on hair in its active growth phase; the rest needs time to get there. So the second session comes no sooner than two months after the first. Frequent visits do not speed the result up.

What it feels like

A noticeable tingling is normal. Burning is not: if the skin burns, the settings are wrong. In the intimate area we work slower and gentler, and adjust the power from session to session.

How to prepare

Shave the area a day before. Avoid the sun for two weeks. For a month before, do not pluck, wax or sugar — the hair has to stay in the follicle, otherwise the laser has nothing to act on.
